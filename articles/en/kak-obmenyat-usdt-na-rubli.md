# How to exchange USDT to rubles

USDT is the most popular stablecoin for cashing out to rubles: the rate is pegged to the dollar, and the
TRC20 network has a low transfer fee. Below is how to exchange via an [exchanger monitor](/en/o-servise/)
and what to watch for.

## Step 1. Determine the USDT network

USDT exists on [different networks](/en/blog/usdt-seti-trc20-erc20-bep20/) — TRC20, ERC20, BEP20, etc. For
cashing out to rubles people most often use **[USDT TRC20](/en/valuta/tether-trc20/)**: a low, stable
network fee. Make sure your coins are on the exact network the exchanger accepts.

## Step 2. Pick a direction and payout method

Decide how you want to receive rubles: to a bank card (e.g. [Sberbank](/en/valuta/sberbank/) or
[T-Bank](/en/valuta/tinkoff/)), via instant payments or cash. Then open the needed exchange direction.

## Step 3. Compare exchangers

In the monitor compare the rate, **reserve** (is there enough for your amount), rating and limits. The top
rate is not always the best — factor in reserve and minimum. How to choose — in the article
[how to choose an exchanger](/en/blog/kak-vybrat-obmennik/).

## Step 4. (Crypto) Do an AML check if needed

If the amount is large, before the operation it makes sense to do an
[AML check](/en/blog/chto-takoe-aml-proverka/) of the address to reduce the risk of a freeze.

## Step 5. Complete the exchange on the exchanger's site

Go to the chosen office, enter the details for receiving rubles and the address/network for sending USDT,
follow the exchanger's instructions. The exchange happens on their side — our site just helps compare rates.

## Common mistakes

- **Wrong network.** Sending USDT TRC20 to an ERC20 address can lose funds. Match the network.
- **Ignoring the reserve.** The rate is great, but there isn't enough reserve for your amount.
- **Forgetting the network fee.** Final amount = rate − [network fee](/en/blog/komissii-setey-tron-eth-bsc/) − exchanger fee.

## In short

- For cashing out to rubles people usually take USDT TRC20 (a cheap network).
- Compare the rate together with reserve, limits and rating.
- Match the network on both sides and account for all fees.
